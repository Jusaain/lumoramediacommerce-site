Lumora Media Commerce

Lumora Media Commerce is the internal Google Ads analytics, campaign reporting, and Keyword Planner research application operated by Lumora Media LLC. It connects our authorized Google Ads Manager Accounts to monitor campaign performance, analyze historical search demand, and plan product assortments for our owned direct-to-consumer e-commerce brands.

About the Lumora Media Commerce Application

Lumora Media Commerce is a Google Ads API reporting and Keyword Planner analytics application operated by Lumora Media LLC. All product information, company details, and privacy disclosures are publicly available on this website with no login required.

What the Application Does

Connects via Google OAuth 2.0 (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords) to authorized Google Ads Manager Accounts to read account metadata, campaign performance statistics, historical Keyword Planner search volumes, and keyword ideas.

Strict Data & Scope Boundaries

Used strictly for numerical advertising analytics and search-demand research for Lumora Media LLC's own retail accounts. The application does not create, upload, edit, host, or process photos, videos, personal media, or user-generated imagery.

Transparent Privacy & Control

Full details on Google user data access, storage, retention, Limited Use compliance, and revocation are published openly in our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
