Lumora Media Commerce

E-Commerce Advertising Analytics & Keyword Demand Research

Lumora Media Commerce is an advertising analytics, campaign performance reporting, and Google Ads Keyword Planner research application created by Lumora Media LLC. It helps retail merchandising and marketing teams measure Google Shopping and Search profitability, benchmark seasonal keyword demand across international markets, and plan product assortments with real search volume data.

Core Capabilities of Lumora Media Commerce

Built for direct-to-consumer e-commerce operations across the United States, United Kingdom, Germany, Switzerland, and Australia, Lumora Media Commerce turns structured Google Ads data into clear merchandising, bidding, and budget decisions.

Keyword Planner Demand Research

Retrieves historical monthly search volumes, 12-month seasonality trends, competition indexes, and historical average CPC benchmarks via the Google Ads Keyword Planner API to measure commercial product demand by country and language.

Keyword Idea Discovery

Discovers related commercial search queries from product category seeds to compare regional phrasing, seasonal demand peaks, and long-tail product opportunities before launching new retail collections.

Campaign & Shopping Reporting

Summarizes campaign, ad group, search term, and Shopping product-group metrics through Google Ads Query Language (GAQL) to track return on ad spend (ROAS), click-through rate, conversion rate, and cost per conversion.

Multi-Market Currency & Geo Normalization

Standardizes reporting across USD, GBP, EUR, CHF, and AUD markets with accurate Google geographic target constants and hemisphere-aware seasonal windows.

Search Term & Negative Keyword Audits

Compares delivered search queries against store categories to spot non-commercial or irrelevant traffic early and keep Shopping and Search campaigns focused on high-intent buyers.

Catalog & Feed Alignment

Matches product-level advertising metrics with regional search demand so catalog titles, categories, and inventory priorities reflect real buyer demand.

How Lumora Media Commerce Works

Lumora Media Commerce operates as a desktop and reporting application using the official Google Ads REST API to analyze advertising performance and search volume data.

Step 1

Connect Google Ads Data

Connects to managed Google Ads profiles using the official Google Ads API scope (adwords) with full user consent.

Step 2

Retrieve Performance Metrics

Reads structured campaign, product-group, and search-term statistics (impressions, clicks, cost, conversions, conversion value) via GAQL.

Step 3

Benchmark Keyword Demand

Queries generateKeywordHistoricalMetrics and generateKeywordIdeas to evaluate 12-month search demand and CPC benchmarks by target market.

Step 4

Deliver Actionable Reports

Exports clean JSON and spreadsheet summaries for assortment planning, seasonal budget allocation, and product title optimization.
