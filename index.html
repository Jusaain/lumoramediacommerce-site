Lumora Media Commerce

Lumora Media Commerce is an e-commerce advertising analytics, campaign reporting, and Keyword Planner search-demand application built and operated by Lumora Media LLC. It connects authorized Google Ads Manager Accounts via the Google Ads API to measure shopping and search campaign profitability, benchmark seasonal keyword demand across international markets, and coordinate product catalog merchandising.

Core Capabilities of Lumora Media Commerce

Lumora Media LLC operates direct-to-consumer online retail brands across the United States, United Kingdom, Germany, Switzerland, and Australia. Lumora Media Commerce provides structured campaign telemetry and search-market data to guide merchandising, bidding, and inventory decisions.

Keyword Planner Demand Research

Queries historical monthly search volumes, 12-month seasonality curves, competition levels, and historical average CPC benchmarks through the Google Ads Keyword Planner API to identify commercial product demand by country and language.

Keyword Idea Discovery

Generates related commercial search queries from curated category seeds to evaluate regional language variations, seasonal demand shifts, and long-tail product opportunities before expanding catalog assortments.

Campaign & Product-Level Reporting

Reads campaign, ad group, search term, and Shopping product-group metrics via Google Ads Query Language (GAQL) to calculate return on ad spend (ROAS), click-through rate, conversion rate, and cost per acquisition.

Multi-Market Currency & Geo Normalization

Aligns account currencies (USD, GBP, EUR, CHF, AUD), geographic target constants, and hemisphere seasonality across separate regional storefront accounts managed under one Google Ads Manager Account.

Search Term & Negative Keyword Auditing

Reviews delivered search queries against catalog taxonomy to surface irrelevant or non-commercial traffic, supporting clean campaign structures and efficient advertising spend allocation.

Feed & Assortment Alignment

Compares live Shopping product performance against Merchant Center feed attributes and regional search demand so high-demand items stay accurately titled, categorized, and stocked.

How Lumora Media Commerce Works

The application runs as a desktop and server-side analytics tool for Lumora Media LLC's advertising team, using standard OAuth 2.0 authorization with the official Google Ads REST API.

Step 1

OAuth 2.0 Authorization

An authorized Lumora Media LLC Google Ads administrator grants permission via Google OAuth 2.0 using the adwords scope to connect our Manager Account.

Step 2

Account & Metric Retrieval

The tool lists accessible Google Ads accounts under the Manager Account and pulls structured performance tables (impressions, clicks, cost, conversions, conversion value).

Step 3

Keyword Demand Lookup

Using generateKeywordHistoricalMetrics and generateKeywordIdeas, the application evaluates search volume and CPC ranges for candidate product categories.

Step 4

Structured Reporting

Outputs clean JSON and spreadsheet reports used by Lumora Media LLC to optimize ad budgets, refine product titles, and plan seasonal retail assortments.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who operates Lumora Media Commerce?

Lumora Media Commerce is operated by Lumora Media LLC, a Wyoming Limited Liability Company headquartered at 30 N Gould St Ste N, Sheridan, WY 82801, United States.

What is the primary purpose of the app?

The application automates Google Ads performance reporting, Shopping campaign diagnostics, and Keyword Planner search volume research for retail brands owned by Lumora Media LLC.

Does the website require an account to read documentation?

No. All information about Lumora Media Commerce, our Google Ads API scope, our Privacy Policy, and our Terms of Service is openly accessible on this website.

How can OAuth access be revoked?

Authorized Google account holders can revoke Lumora Media Commerce's API permissions at any time in their Google Account Third-Party Connections settings or request deletion via info@lumoramediacommerce.com.

Which geographic markets are supported?

Our retail and analytics workflows cover North America, Europe (Germany, Switzerland, United Kingdom), and Oceania (Australia), using localized Google Ads geo-target and language constants.

How does Lumora Media Commerce protect API data?

All API communication uses TLS encryption, OAuth 2.0 with PKCE, and least-privilege credentials. No customer payment card data or unrelated personal Google workspace data is accessed.
