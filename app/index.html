Lumora Media Commerce

E-commerce advertising analytics and search-demand research for Google Shopping and Search, across five international markets.

Lumora Media Commerce is reporting software operated by Lumora Media LLC. It connects to the official Google Ads REST API through Google OAuth 2.0 to report campaign and Shopping performance with GAQL, and to benchmark search demand and cost-per-click with Google Ads Keyword Planner data in the United States, United Kingdom, Germany, Switzerland and Australia.

It requests one OAuth scope, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords, uses the data only to produce reports, never sells or shares it, and can be disconnected at any time from your Google Account permissions.

01 / Capabilities

Six reporting modules, one consistent view of every market.

Each module maps to a documented Google Ads API resource or service. Results are normalized into one currency-aware structure so that a Shopping campaign in Zurich can be compared with a Search campaign in Sydney on equal terms.

M1

Campaign performance reporting

Daily campaign, ad group and network performance retrieved with Google Ads Query Language through googleAds:searchStream.

  • Impressions, clicks, cost, conversions, conversion value
  • Date, device and network segmentation
  • Period-over-period comparison
M2

Shopping product attribution

Item-level Google Shopping results from shopping_performance_view, grouped by product type, brand and custom label.

  • Product item ID and title level
  • Product-group and custom-label rollups
  • Zero-sale and high-cost item detection
M3

Search-term intent analysis

Search-term reports from search_term_view classified by purchase intent to separate buying queries from research traffic.

  • Transactional, commercial, informational tiers
  • N-gram cost aggregation
  • Protected purchase-keyword lists
M4

Keyword idea discovery

Related keyword ideas from the Keyword Planner method generateKeywordIdeas, seeded with category terms per market and language.

  • Seed keywords or landing-page URL
  • Market-specific geo and language constants
  • Deduplicated, intent-tagged idea lists
M5

Search volume & CPC benchmarks

Historical metrics per keyword: average monthly searches, a 12-month volume series, competition and top-of-page bid ranges.

  • avgMonthlySearches and monthly volumes
  • competitionIndex 0 to 100
  • Top-of-page bid range and average CPC
M6

Currency & seasonality normalization

Micros converted to USD, GBP, EUR, CHF and AUD, with a 12-month seasonality index that respects northern and southern hemisphere calendars.

  • Native and USD-equivalent reporting
  • Seasonality index per market
  • Structured JSON and CSV exports
02 / Operational workflow

From authorization to report in four controlled steps.

Every request travels over encrypted HTTPS to official Google endpoints. Credentials stay inside an access-controlled environment, and the only output is a structured report.

Step 01

Authorize

An authorized Google Ads administrator approves the Google consent screen. The OAuth 2.0 authorization code flow uses PKCE (S256) and requests the single adwords scope.

Step 02

Query

Reporting modules send GAQL queries to GoogleAdsService. Research modules call KeywordPlanIdeaService for keyword ideas and historical metrics.

Step 03

Normalize

Cost micros are converted to currency units, markets are tagged with geo and language constants, and monthly volumes become a 12-month seasonality index.

Step 04

Report

Results are written as structured JSON and CSV reporting exports with restricted file permissions. Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.

03 / International markets

Five markets, two hemispheres, one reporting model.

Each market is defined by its Google Ads geo target constant, language constant and account currency. Seasonal calendars differ: Australia's summer falls in December and January, so demand curves are planned per hemisphere rather than copied across markets.

01What is Lumora Media Commerce?

Lumora Media Commerce is e-commerce advertising analytics software. It reports Google Shopping and Search campaign performance with GAQL and researches search demand and cost-per-click with Google Ads Keyword Planner data, across the United States, United Kingdom, Germany, Switzerland and Australia.

02Who operates Lumora Media Commerce?

Lumora Media LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company with its registered office at 30 N Gould St Ste N, Sheridan, WY 82801, United States. Lumora Media LLC is the data controller for the application and this website.

03Who uses the application?

Lumora Media Commerce is used by Lumora Media LLC and authorized Google Ads administrators to report on Google Ads accounts they own or are authorized to manage. There is no public registration; every connection is approved by a Google Ads administrator on Google's own consent screen.

04Which Google OAuth scope is requested, and why?

Only https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords. It is the scope the Google Ads API requires to read campaign reports and to call Keyword Planner. No other Google scope is requested.

05Which data does it access?

Google Ads account metadata (customer ID, descriptive name, currency, time zone), campaign, ad group, Shopping product and search-term performance statistics, and Keyword Planner metrics such as average monthly searches, 12-month volumes, competition and bid ranges. It does not access Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Contacts, payment details or end-customer personal data.

06Is Google user data sold, shared or used for advertising?

No. Google user data is never sold, rented or shared with third parties, never transferred to data brokers, and never used to serve or target advertising. Use and transfer follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

07How can access be revoked and data deleted?

Remove Lumora Media Commerce at myaccount.google.com/permissions; access ends immediately. To delete stored reporting data, email info@lumoramediacommerce.com. Verified requests are completed within 30 days.

08Does this website use cookies or tracking?

No. This website is static HTML and CSS. It sets no cookies, loads no analytics or advertising scripts and contains no tracking pixels. Hosting providers process standard request logs only to deliver and secure the pages.

06 / Corporate identity

Lumora Media Commerce is a product of Lumora Media LLC.

Questions about the application, Google API data handling, privacy or deletion requests reach the operating company directly. There are no intermediaries.
