Effective Date: November 24, 2026 · Lumora Media LLC

This Privacy Policy explains how Lumora Media LLC ("Lumora Media Commerce", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our corporate website lumoramediacommerce.com and our internal Lumora Media Commerce analytics application.

1. Data Controller

Lumora Media LLC

2. Website Information

Our corporate website is informational and does not require account registration. Standard server access logs (such as IP address, timestamp, browser type, and requested URL) may be processed temporarily by our hosting provider solely for security, stability, and delivery of the website. If you contact us by email, we use your contact details solely to respond to your inquiry.

3. Google User Data & Google API Services Disclosure

The Lumora Media Commerce application uses Google OAuth 2.0 to access the Google Ads API strictly for internal advertising analytics and keyword research of Lumora Media LLC.

  • Requested Google OAuth Scope: We request only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords scope. We do not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Google Photos, Google Contacts, or other personal user data scopes.
  • Google User Data Accessed: Authorized Google Ads account identifiers, campaign/ad group performance metrics, and Google Ads Keyword Planner historical search volume metrics, CPC estimates, and keyword ideas.
  • How Google User Data Is Used: Exclusively to display campaign performance reports and evaluate commercial keyword search demand for e-commerce brands owned and operated by Lumora Media LLC.
  • No Sale or Third-Party Transfer: We never sell, rent, disclose, or transfer Google user data to third parties, data brokers, or external advertising networks.
  • No Media or AI Model Training: Google user data is never used to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models, and our application does not process, host, or generate personal photos or videos.
  • Limited Use Compliance: Lumora Media Commerce's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs strictly adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Storage & Security

OAuth credentials and campaign reporting data are stored on access-controlled systems with encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS) and restricted file permissions accessible only to authorized Lumora Media LLC personnel.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain operational reporting data only as long as required for internal business analytics and statutory recordkeeping. Authorized users may revoke OAuth access at any time through their Google Account security settings or request deletion of stored records by emailing info@lumoramediacommerce.com.

6. Contact

For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact info@lumoramediacommerce.com.
