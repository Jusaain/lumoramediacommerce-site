4. Cookies, Analytics, and Third-Party Trackers

Our corporate website sets no advertising cookies, tracking pixels, behavioral profiling scripts, or third-party analytics trackers. Static styles and content are served directly from our domain without contacting external marketing networks.

5. Google User Data and Google API Services Disclosure

The Lumora Media Commerce application integrates with the official Google Ads API through Google OAuth 2.0 to support advertising reporting and keyword demand research for Lumora Media LLC's own e-commerce brands.

  • Requested Google OAuth Scope: Lumora Media Commerce requests exclusively the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords scope. We do not request access to Gmail messages, Google Drive files, Google Calendar, Google Contacts, or other unrelated Google account services.
  • Categories of Google User Data Accessed: Authorized Google Ads Manager and client account metadata (customer IDs, descriptive names, currency, time zone), campaign, ad group, product group, and search term performance statistics (impressions, clicks, cost, conversions, conversion value), as well as Google Ads Keyword Planner historical monthly search volumes, average CPC metrics, competition indexes, and keyword ideas.
  • Purpose of Data Use: Accessed data is used solely to generate internal campaign performance reports, monitor advertising return on investment, audit Shopping product feeds, and plan seasonal retail assortments for Lumora Media LLC.
  • No Sale or Unauthorized Disclosure: We never sell, rent, lease, or distribute Google user data to third parties, data brokers, or external advertising platforms.
  • Strict Limited Use Compliance: Lumora Media Commerce's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Email Communications

If you contact us via info@lumoramediacommerce.com, we process your email address, name, and message content solely to review and respond to your inquiry and to maintain ordinary business correspondence records.

7. Legal Bases for Processing

Where the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), UK GDPR, or Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) applies, we process personal data on the following legal bases: legitimate interests in secure website delivery and internal business analytics (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), performance of a contract or pre-contractual communication (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), and explicit authorization via Google OAuth 2.0 consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

8. Data Security and Storage Practices

We implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect stored data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. All Google API communication uses HTTPS/TLS encryption and OAuth 2.0 authorization with PKCE. Stored OAuth credentials and exported campaign reports are kept on access-controlled systems with restricted file permissions (0600) accessible only to authorized Lumora Media LLC personnel.

9. Data Retention, Revocation, and Deletion

We retain campaign performance reports and keyword research exports only as long as required for business reporting, year-over-year seasonality comparisons, and statutory accounting obligations. Business email correspondence is retained in accordance with applicable commercial retention rules.

Any authorized Google account user may revoke Lumora Media Commerce's API access immediately at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Upon revocation, our application can no longer refresh tokens or access Google Ads data. To request deletion of previously exported reporting data or correspondence, email info@lumoramediacommerce.com; verified deletion requests are completed within 30 days.

Depending on your jurisdiction (including GDPR, UK GDPR, FADP, and applicable US state privacy laws), you may have the right to request access to, correction of, erasure of, restriction of, or portability of your personal data, to object to processing, to withdraw consent at any time, and to lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority.

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect operational, technical, or regulatory changes. The effective date at the top of this page indicates the most recent revision. Questions regarding privacy or data protection can be sent at any time to info@lumoramediacommerce.com.
