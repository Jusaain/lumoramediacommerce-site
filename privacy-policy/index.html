Lumora Media Commerce Privacy Policy

01Who we are

Lumora Media Commerce is an e-commerce advertising analytics application. It reports Google Shopping and Search campaign performance using the Google Ads Query Language (GAQL) and researches search demand and cost-per-click benchmarks using Google Ads Keyword Planner data, through the official Google Ads API.

Lumora Media Commerce is operated by Lumora Media LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company, which is responsible for the processing of personal data described in this policy (the "controller"). In this policy, "Lumora Media Commerce", "we", "us" and "our" refer to Lumora Media LLC and its application.

02Scope of this policy

This policy applies to:

  • Website visitors who view pages on www.lumoramediacommerce.com or web.lumoramediacommerce.com;
  • Authorized Google Ads users who connect a Google Ads account to Lumora Media Commerce through Google OAuth 2.0 and thereby allow it to access Google Ads data; and
  • Correspondents who contact us by email.

This policy does not apply to Google's own processing of your data. Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

03Information we collect

3.1 Website request data

When you view this website, your browser automatically sends standard technical information to the hosting servers: IP address, date and time of the request, requested URL, HTTP status code, referring URL, browser user agent and the amount of data transferred. This information is used only to deliver pages, maintain security and prevent abuse. We do not combine it with other data and do not use it to identify or profile visitors.

3.2 Google Ads data accessed through Google APIs

When an authorized user connects a Google Ads account, Lumora Media Commerce accesses the Google Ads data described in Section 4 under the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords scope.

3.3 OAuth credentials

The authorization process issues an OAuth access token and refresh token. We store these tokens securely so that reports can be generated without asking you to authorize again. We never receive or store your Google password.

3.4 Email correspondence

If you write to us, we process your email address, name, the content of your message and any information you choose to include, in order to respond and to keep ordinary business correspondence records.

We do not collect special categories of personal data, payment card data or information about children.

04Google user data: what we access and what we do not

OAuth scope requested. Lumora Media Commerce requests exactly one Google OAuth scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords, which provides access to the Google Ads API. We do not request any other Google scope.

4.3 Search terms

Search-term reports contain the text of queries that triggered ads as reported by Google, in aggregated form. Google applies its own privacy thresholds to these reports. We do not attempt to link search terms to individual people.

05How we use information

We use Google user data only to provide the reporting and research features of Lumora Media Commerce to the user who authorized access:

  • generating campaign, Shopping product and search-term performance reports;
  • calculating derived metrics such as click-through rate, cost per click, cost per acquisition and return on ad spend;
  • converting values between the supported currencies (USD, GBP, EUR, CHF, AUD) and comparing the supported markets (United States, United Kingdom, Germany, Switzerland, Australia);
  • researching search demand, seasonality and cost-per-click benchmarks with Keyword Planner data; and
  • producing structured JSON and CSV reporting exports for the authorizing user.

06Google API Services User Data Policy & Limited Use

Lumora Media Commerce's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

We also comply with the Google APIs Terms of Service. Specifically:

  • We use Google user data only to provide or improve user-facing features that are prominent in the application and described in this policy.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to others unless necessary to provide or improve those features, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets with notice to users.
  • We do not use or transfer Google user data for serving advertisements, including retargeting, personalized or interest-based advertising.
  • We do not sell Google user data and do not use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.
  • We do not allow humans to read Google user data unless we have your affirmative agreement for specific data, it is necessary for security purposes such as investigating abuse, it is necessary to comply with applicable law, or the data is aggregated and anonymized and used for internal operations.

07Sharing & disclosure

We do not sell, rent, lease or trade personal data or Google user data, and we do not share it with data brokers, advertising networks or other third parties for their own purposes.

We disclose information only in the following limited cases:

  • Infrastructure providers that host this website and our systems and process data only on our instructions: GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages hosting for www.lumoramediacommerce.com), the provider of the dedicated server that hosts web.lumoramediacommerce.com and the application, and Squarespace Domains II LLC (domain registration and DNS). These providers do not receive Google user data for their own use.
  • Google, as the provider of the Google Ads API, receives the API requests that are necessary to retrieve your data.
  • Legal requirements: where disclosure is required by applicable law, regulation, legal process or an enforceable governmental request, or is necessary to protect the rights, property or safety of Lumora Media LLC, our users or the public.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to this policy and with notice to affected users.

08Cookies, logs & tracking technologies

This website sets no cookies. It uses no analytics tools, no advertising or social-media pixels, no fingerprinting and no third-party scripts. Pages consist of static HTML, CSS and images served from our own domain.

Our hosting providers keep standard server request logs (see Section 3.1) for security and operations. Logs on our own server are retained for a limited period, typically no longer than 30 days, unless needed to investigate a specific security incident. Logs at GitHub Pages are governed by GitHub's privacy statement.

Because no tracking takes place, no "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signal changes how this website behaves; such signals are respected by design.

09Data security

We maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the nature of the data, including:

  • OAuth 2.0 authorization code flow with PKCE (S256); we never handle Google passwords;
  • encryption in transit with HTTPS using TLS 1.2 or 1.3 for all communication with Google APIs and for this website;
  • storage of OAuth tokens, credentials and reporting exports on access-controlled systems in files with permission mode 0600;
  • no credentials in source code, browsers, logs or exports;
  • access limited to authorized Lumora Media LLC personnel on a need-to-know basis; and
  • prompt revocation and rotation of credentials if a compromise is suspected.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If we become aware of a security incident affecting your personal data, we will notify you and the competent authorities as required by applicable law. More detail is available on our Security page.

10Data retention, revocation & deletion

10.1 Retention

  • Google Ads reporting data and exports are kept only as long as needed for reporting, year-over-year seasonality comparison and statutory record-keeping, and are deleted when no longer needed or upon a verified deletion request.
  • OAuth tokens are kept while the connection is active and deleted when access is revoked or the connection is ended.
  • Server request logs are kept for a limited period, typically no longer than 30 days.
  • Email correspondence is kept for as long as necessary to handle the inquiry and to meet commercial record-keeping obligations.

10.2 Revoking access

You can revoke Lumora Media Commerce's access to your Google account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revocation takes effect immediately: the application can no longer refresh tokens or retrieve any Google Ads data.

10.3 Requesting deletion

To have stored data deleted, send an email to info@lumoramediacommerce.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and the Google Ads customer ID or email address concerned. We will verify the request, delete the OAuth tokens, reporting data and exports associated with it within 30 days, and confirm completion in writing. Data we are legally required to keep is retained only for that purpose and deleted when the obligation ends.

11Your privacy rights

11.1 EU, UK and Swiss residents (GDPR, UK GDPR, FADP)

We process personal data on the following legal bases: your consent given through the Google OAuth consent screen (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); the performance of a contract or steps prior to entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)); compliance with legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)); and our legitimate interests in operating, securing and improving our website and services (Art. 6(1)(f)).

You have the right to access your personal data, to rectify inaccurate data, to erasure, to restrict processing, to data portability, to object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. You may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, such as the authority in your country of residence, the UK Information Commissioner's Office or the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner.

11.2 International transfers

Lumora Media LLC is established in the United States. Personal data may be processed in the United States and the European Union. Where personal data from the EU, UK or Switzerland is transferred to countries without an adequacy decision, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and their UK and Swiss equivalents.

11.3 US state privacy rights

Depending on your state of residence, including California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah and other states with comprehensive privacy laws, you may have the right to know what personal information we process, to access, correct and delete it, to obtain a portable copy, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising. We do not sell or share personal information and do not use it for targeted advertising. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. You may use an authorized agent to submit a request.

11.4 Exercising your rights

Send your request to info@lumoramediacommerce.com. We may ask for information to verify your identity and will respond within the period required by applicable law, generally within 30 days (45 days under applicable US state laws). If we decline a request, you may appeal by replying to our decision.

11.5 Children

Lumora Media Commerce is a business tool and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16 years of age.

If we make material changes, in particular to how we use Google user data, we will notify affected users before the changes take effect and, where required, ask for renewed consent.
