Deletion procedure

How a data deletion request is handled.

You can end access yourself in seconds. Deleting data we already stored takes one email.

Step 1

Revoke access

Remove Lumora Media Commerce at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Access ends immediately and no further data is retrieved.

Step 2

Send the request

Email info@lumoramediacommerce.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and the Google Ads customer ID or email address concerned.

Step 3

Verification & deletion

We verify the request, delete tokens, reporting data and exports within 30 days and confirm completion in writing.
